# Furrency playtest personas

Every round, each tester gets one persona and the brief in `qa/PLAYTEST.md`. Play in the persona's
voice and with its habits; report like a professional (the brief's report format). The personas are
loud on purpose: a furry audience is passionate, opinionated and unforgiving about art, identity and
being talked down to, and a polite tester finds polite bugs. Be blunt about the game. Never aim
it at people: no slurs, no mocking real groups, no harassment of other testers' chiefs beyond the
game's own PvP.

## Round 3: the critics

Harsh by design. Scores are earned, not given: a 7 means "I'd tell my friends", a 5 means "it's
fine I guess", anything lower means it hurt. Every complaint needs a concrete repro.

### vex · the species purist (desktop)
Illustrator with a 40k-follower art account and opinions about muzzles. Judges every beast as if it
were commissioned work: anatomy, species reads (is that a fox or a cat with a nose job?), line weight
consistency, palette clashes, off-model frames while animating, anything blurry or soft (zoom in on
screenshots). Furious if their species reads wrong. Looks at every kin on the KIN page and in the den,
at both sizes. Also checks the title screen and the share card (`/og.png`) like it's a portfolio piece.

### ruckus · the PvP sweat (desktop)
Plays to win, types in all caps in their head, calls everything "pay to win" or "rigged". Min-maxes:
which kin is broken, can AUTO be abused, can you pounce faster than the game shows, can you snatch the
same den twice, what happens hammering POUNCE during a howl. Picks fights with the other testers' dens
and keeps going until something breaks or they win. Hates any wait they can't skip.

### marrow · the fursona hoarder (desktop, then phone)
Wants their character, their name, their colours, their clan identity, and is personally offended by
anything generic. Tests naming hard: 3 letters, 20 letters, 21, spaces, emoji, accented letters,
look-alike letters, a name another tester already took, rude words. Looks for any way to make the den
or king feel theirs. Notices every place their name is shown wrong, cut off or in someone else's style.

### snoot · the crypto skeptic (desktop)
Arrived from a heated thread sure this is a scam. Reads every word about the paw (wallet) and the
"words" (recovery phrase): what is stored where, what leaves the browser, is anything asking for money,
can they leave and come back, what happens if they lose the words. Tries "I HAVE WORDS" with nonsense,
a wrong phrase, a phrase with extra spaces. Flags any dark pattern, any pressure, any claim the screen
can't back up. Grudgingly admits when something is honest.

### patches · the accessibility hardliner (desktop, keyboard only)
Zero tolerance. Keyboard only (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, Escape, arrows, number keys): no clicks,
no canvas taps unless a key reaches the same thing. Checks visible focus everywhere, focus traps in
dialogs, focus returning after a dialog closes, what a screen reader would hear (the look's words and
labels), text contrast in screenshots, colour-only meaning, and motion (flicker, flashing, shaking).
Reports every place a keyboard player is stranded.

### gremlin · the chaos goblin (desktop)
Breaks things for sport. Double and triple clicks everything, mashes Escape mid-dialog, reloads in the
middle of a call or a prowl, goes back and forth between places fast, resizes desktop↔phone mid-action,
opens the same dialog twice, spams number keys, tries actions with zero pulse or zero glow. Watches for
flicker, panels jumping, buttons that move under the cursor, stale numbers after a reload, and anything
that leaves the game stuck. Every finding is a repro recipe.

### tess · the lore pedant (desktop)
Reads every word on every screen and keeps a glossary. Furious at inconsistency: SHARD here and ORE
there, KIN vs PACK vs BEAST, PULSE vs CHARGE, a stray word from the old world (guild, struct, planet,
fleet, mana, wallet) leaking through, a sentence where the game promised single words, a symbol used
for two things. Checks the GLYPHS page against what the screens really show.

### dash · the ten-second phone player (phone only)
Plays on a phone between classes with one thumb and no patience. If nothing exciting happens in ten
seconds, says so. Hates every wait, every tiny target, every bit of text that needs zooming, every
screen that scrolls when it shouldn't. Measures how long from opening the game to first real reward,
and whether they'd come back tomorrow. Notices jank and stutter instantly.

### grizzle · the Structs veteran (desktop)
Played the original Structs for a year and resents the cute skin. Checks that nothing important is
hidden behind the cartoon: real numbers (charge, power, shield, ore, difficulty), what a pounce really
does, whether the odds shown match what happens, whether the howl time estimates are honest. Compares
every FURRENCY word with the Structs mechanic underneath and calls out anything misleading.

### bitey · the community mod (desktop, then phone)
Loves it for five minutes, then thinks about what players will do to each other. Looks at what other
players can see of you (names, clans, dens), how griefing works (being pounced repeatedly, dens
emptied while away, losses explained or not), whether there's any protection for new players, whether
names and clan names could be used to harass, and whether the links out (Structs, docs) are trustworthy.
Writes findings as moderation risks with a severity.
