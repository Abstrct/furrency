Furrency — Be the Alpha

Furrency is a free comic-book strategy game of majestic anthropomorphic fur beasts with cyber eyes, played in the browser on desktop and phone. Every den, every beast and every blow is real: the game runs live on the Structs blockchain network, and every rival chief is a real player.

How to play

  1. Join: make a paw (your key, kept in your browser), name your chief and pick a clan. You get a den and a Sun Lion king.
  2. Dig: call a Dig Mole to dig shards out of your den.
  3. Forge: call a Forge Badger to forge shards into alpha, the one coin. Shards can be stolen; alpha cannot.
  4. Glow: feed alpha to your clan's sun for glow, the power that lets you call more kin.
  5. Hunt and pounce: find rival dens holding shards, prowl there with your pack, pounce their king and snatch their shards.
  6. Howl: every call, dig, forge and snatch is sealed by a howl, a proof of work your browser sings.

The kin

Sun Lion (king), Star Bear, Comet Fox, Void Owl, Gust Ferret, Shade Bat, Storm Hare, Boulder Boar, Iron Rhino, Quill Hog, Tide Otter, Fang Walrus, Deep Mink — and the totems: Dig Mole, Forge Badger, Star Pole, Hush Moon, Stone Burrow, Thorn Spire, Glow Stone, Glow Heart, Sun Tree. They stand in four zones: Star, Sky, Root and Tide.
